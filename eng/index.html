Holiday marketing

I’ll show you how my “2+1” promo only worked on people who would’ve come anyway, and how to build a Black Friday and New Year offer that solves your client’s problem instead of just being cheaper. You’ll build your own offer live on the stream and get story and mailing templates.

And how much does one client cost me?

AI doesn’t know how much one client costs you, whether your front desk will call the people who came for the promo, or what to do when the promo brought in people who would’ve come anyway.

It happened to me: my “2+1” promo “worked”, but it was only bought by regulars who would’ve come anyway.

So in the intensive, I won’t give you yet another list of promos. I’ll show you how to think about a promo like an owner: how many clients you need, exactly who you want to attract, and what happens to them after the first visit.
